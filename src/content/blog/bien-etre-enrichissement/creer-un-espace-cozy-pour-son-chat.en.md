# Creating a cozy space for your cat: interior design

Your cat deserves a comfortable and safe haven in your home. Designing a space tailored to its natural needs enhances its well-being and strengthens your bond.

## Hiding spots: an essential refuge

Cats love to hide to feel secure. Provide cozy hiding spots: a closed box with an entrance hole, a fabric teepee, or a space under a low piece of furniture. Plush cushions or soft rugs near a wall also create reassuring nooks. Vary the heights and textures to stimulate its curiosity while offering it intimate retreats.

## Height: a strategic vantage point

Offering high-up vantage points is crucial for your feline. Install wall shelves, a cat tree, or a secure windowsill. Cats love observing their environment from above, which gives them a sense of control and security. A window corner with a soft rug transforms a simple space into a prime spot for basking in the sun or keeping an eye on the outdoors.

## Rest areas: warmth and comfort

Set up rest areas in the rooms where you spend time. A cushion near the radiator (without direct contact) or a blanket on a cozy armchair invites your cat to rest beside you. Heated beds or faux fur cushions are perfect for cats that love warmth. Avoid drafts and prefer calm locations to maximize their comfort.

## Access to light and nature

Cats are drawn to natural light. Arrange a corner near a window with a rug or a blanket so it can enjoy the sun. A small shelf near the window will offer it an ideal observation post. Non-toxic plants like the spider plant or cactus can decorate the space safely for it.

### FAQ

**Q: What is the best place to install a cat bed?**  
A: Choose a quiet spot, sheltered from drafts, and close to frequently visited areas like the living room or bedroom. Cats like to be close to their family while still feeling secure.

**Q: Should I plan several cozy spaces in the house?**  
A: Yes, multiplying rest areas and hiding spots allows your cat to choose according to its mood and the temperature. One per level or per room is ideal to cover all its needs.
