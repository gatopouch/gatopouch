# Pet Carriers: Choosing the Right One for Your Cat

Transporting your cat with peace of mind requires a carrier that is suited to its needs. Discover how to select the ideal model for safe and comfortable travel.

## Size: An Essential Criterion

The size of the carrier is the first element to consider. It must allow your cat to stand up, turn around, and lie down comfortably. For small breeds, a 50x30x30 cm carrier is sufficient, while larger cats require a minimum of 70x45x40 cm. Opt for models with an opening on the top or side to make it easier to place the cat inside. A carrier that is too large can be uncomfortable, while one that is too small causes stress.

## Material: Durability and Practicality

Rigid plastic carriers are the most robust and easy to clean, ideal for car or air travel. Fabric models are lighter and foldable, perfect for short trips or vet visits. Ensure the material is resistant to scratches and impacts. Wicker carriers offer a neat aesthetic but require more maintenance. Choose a model with ergonomic handles for easy transport.

## Safety and Ventilation: Absolute Priorities

Ensure the carrier has good ventilation with mesh panels on multiple sides. The doors must be equipped with secure latches to prevent escapes. Models with double locks or nylon fasteners are more reliable. For air travel, check for IATA compliance. A removable, waterproof bottom simplifies cleaning in case of an accident.

## FAQ

**Q: My cat is afraid of the carrier, how can I get it used to it?**  
A: Gradually introduce the carrier in a safe space, with toys and treats inside. Leave it open so he can explore it at his own pace. Associate it with positive moments like meal times.

**Q: Can I use a carrier for air travel?****  
A: Only IATA-certified carriers are permitted in the cabin or hold. Check the dimensions and requirements of the airline. Prefer rigid models with optimal ventilation.
