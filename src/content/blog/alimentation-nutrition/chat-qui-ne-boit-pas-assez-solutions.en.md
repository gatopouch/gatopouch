A well-hydrated cat is a healthy cat, but many felines drink very little, which can lead to kidney or urinary problems. Fortunately, with the right tips and solutions, you can encourage your companion to drink more and ensure they are optimally hydrated.

## Why isn't your cat drinking enough?
Cats originate from arid regions and have developed the ability to extract moisture from their food, but this is not always enough. Several factors can explain why your cat avoids water:

### 1. Natural preference for fresh water
Cats are very sensitive to the freshness of water. Stagnant or lukewarm water can put them off.

### 2. Underlying health problems
Diseases such as urinary tract infections, kidney stones, or diabetes can reduce thirst. Consult a veterinarian if the behavior persists.

### 3. Poor water quality
Cats are sensitive to the taste and smell of chlorine or minerals present in tap water.

### 4. Stress or uncomfortable environment
A change of environment, a bowl that is too small, or a noisy place can discourage your cat from hydrating.

## Effective solutions to encourage hydration
Here are practical methods to stimulate your cat to drink more regularly:

### 1. Offer a water fountain
Water fountains attract cats with the continuous flow that keeps the water fresh and oxygenated. They stimulate their hunting instinct by mimicking a natural stream.

- Choose a model with multiple water flow settings.
- Clean it regularly to prevent bacteria.

### 2. Vary the water sources
Place several bowls of water around the house, away from their food and litter box. Some cats prefer to drink in different locations.

### 3. Use filtered or spring water
Tap water can contain additives that put off your cat. Opt for filtered or spring water for a purer taste.

### 4. Add moisture to their meals
Mix water, unsalted broth, or cooking water (without seasoning) into their kibble or offer wet food that is high in moisture.

## Tips for a stubborn cat
If your cat categorically refuses to drink, these tips can make a difference:

### 1. Freeze water in the summer
Cats love cool water. Ice cubes in their bowl can pique their curiosity.

### 2. Play with water
Use a toy soaked in water or offer a small, shallow pool to stimulate their interest.

### 3. Reward their efforts
Every time your cat drinks, give them a treat or a cuddle to reinforce the positive behavior.

## FAQ: Common Questions About Cat Hydration

### How much water should a cat drink per day?
An adult cat should drink about 30 to 50 ml of water per kilogram of body weight per day. For example, a 4 kg cat needs 120 to 200 ml of water daily.

### My cat is suddenly drinking a lot, is this normal?
A sudden change in water intake can signal a health problem like diabetes or kidney failure. Consult a veterinarian promptly.

### Are water fountains really effective?
Yes, studies show that cats drink more from a water fountain, as the movement and freshness of the water naturally attract them.
