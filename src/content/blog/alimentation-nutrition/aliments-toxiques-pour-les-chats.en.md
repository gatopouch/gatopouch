# Toxic Foods for Cats: Complete List

As a cat owner, it is essential to know the foods that can seriously harm your feline's health. Even some common foods in our kitchens can be dangerous, or even fatal, for them.

## Common Foods to Avoid

Some foods that we consume safely can be toxic to cats. **Chocolate**, especially dark chocolate, contains theobromine, which causes vomiting, seizures, and even cardiac arrest. **Onions, garlic, and leeks**, whether raw or cooked, destroy red blood cells and cause anemia. **Grapes and raisins** can lead to rapid, sometimes irreversible, kidney failure. Finally, **dairy products** are often poorly tolerated due to lactose, causing digestive upset.

## Drinks and Other Dangers

**Alcoholic beverages**, even in small amounts, are extremely dangerous for cats, causing damage to the liver and central nervous system. **Coffee and tea**, due to caffeine, can lead to a rapid heart rate and seizures. Also be careful with **artificial sweeteners**, such as xylitol, found in some chewing gums and candies, which cause severe hypoglycemia and hepatitis.

## Precautions to Take Daily

To prevent accidents, always keep dangerous foods out of your cat's reach. Never leave unattended dishes on the table or countertop. Educate your family members and guests about food restrictions. In case of accidental ingestion, contact your veterinarian or the animal poison control center immediately. Have a list of common toxins on hand in case of an emergency.

## FAQ

**Q: My cat ate a little chocolate, what should I do?**  
A: Even a small amount of dark chocolate is dangerous. Watch for signs (vomiting, agitation) and consult a veterinarian without delay. The larger the amount, the higher the risk.

**Q: Are tomatoes toxic to cats?**  
A: The leaves and tomato plant contain solanine, which is toxic to cats. The ripe pulp is safe, but it's best to avoid giving it regularly.
